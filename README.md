👋 Engineering Profile

I am a Master 2 student in Mechatronics, Energy and Intelligent Systems (MESI) focused on robotics software, computer vision and embedded systems.

I like building complete robotic pipelines rather than isolated algorithms:

Sensors / Cameras
       ↓
   Perception
       ↓
Pose & State Estimation
       ↓
 Planning / Decision
       ↓
     Control
       ↓
  Physical Robot
       ↓
 Test & Validation

Currently, I am working at ABMI Groupe on the integration and validation of a 6-DoF robotic vision demonstrator.
